Play & Connect

Experiences that get your guests talking, laughing, competing, and connecting.

01
A little kindness can change someone's whole day.

Hello World Kindness Station

Celebrate Baby By Being Kind

Guests take a card, read a little reminder, and carry it out into the world in celebration of baby's arrival. Add an optional $5 gift card to any note for a pay it forward surprise.

02
Everyone has a prediction. Only one can be right.

Guess the Arrival

When Will Baby Make Their Grand Entrance?

Guests enter their prediction for Baby's arrival date and time, guess Baby's name and leave a special private message for Mom. The experience continues after the shower with prediction updates, labour notifications and false alarms as the big day approaches.

03
Think you know what babies cost?

The Price Is Right

How Well Do You Know Baby?

From diapers and detergent to strollers and all the things Mom actually registered for, put your pricing skills to the test. Guess the price. Guess where it's cheaper. Guess what Mom bought. Rack up the points. Play individually or team up with friends.

04
How well do you really know Mom and Dad?

Baby Trivia

How Well Do You Really Know Mom and Dad?

A custom round of baby trivia filled with questions about Mom, Dad, their relationship, their baby and the little details only their closest friends and family should know. Some questions will be easy. Some will absolutely expose you.

05
Three stations. One sleepy baby.

Baby Naptime Relay

Can You Get Baby to Sleep?

Race through three stations: bottle chug, diaper change and sing the lullaby. Your lullaby is assigned to you. Sing it correctly to earn your points, finish the course as fast as possible, and prove that you have what it takes to survive bedtime. Fastest caregiver wins.

BOTTLE CHUGDIAPER CHANGESING THE LULLABY
06
Can you fake the bump?

The Maternity Shot Challenge

Can You Fake the Bump?

Grab a pillow. Strike a pose. Convince us you're expecting. Guests compete to create the most convincing, ridiculous, glamorous or downright questionable maternity photo. Scan. Upload. Display. The photos appear on the big screen for everyone to see.

Create & Keep

Experiences where your guests create something meaningful for you to keep.

01
The moments you wish you could bottle up.

Picture This

Capture A Memory, Leave It Behind

Guests capture a photo and leave a handwritten note about their favourite time or story with Mom and Dad, so baby can see what their parents were like when they grow up. Both go into the time capsule to discover someday.

02
Capture the moments Mom will want to remember.

The Photo Challenge

Capture Mom's Big Day

Each guest receives a secret photo challenge with one goal: capture a picture of Mom that fits the assignment. Scan the QR code and add it to the shared album. By the end of the shower, Mom has a whole album of memories from the people who celebrated with her.
