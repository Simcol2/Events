Guess The Arrival

The countdown to baby becomes a celebration of its own.

Guests predict when baby will arrive, where mom will be, what she'll be doing, and what time baby makes their entrance. Guests submit their guesses through a custom digital experience and can sign up for email updates as the big day approaches.

Baby Trivia

How well do they really know the parents-to-be?

We create a custom trivia experience featuring questions about the parents-to-be, their relationship and their story. Guests laugh, compete, and discover something new about the couple they came to celebrate.

Interactive Wall Puzzle

A keepsake built one guest at a time.

Each guest contributes a part of the display throughout the event. A visual reminder that every person there played a part in welcoming this new chapter.

Hello World Kindness Station

A little kindness goes out. Something meaningful stays behind.

Guests pass a kindness card forward and leave genuine words for the host to keep for a rainy day.

Also available: a collaborative Custom Story Book, where guests contribute pages using guided prompts until the pieces become a one-of-a-kind keepsake.
