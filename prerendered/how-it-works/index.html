How It Works

You bring the people. We create the experience.

What is an A Slice of G experience?

Interactive event experiences that become keepsakes. Guests participate, contribute, and create something meaningful during the celebration, and you get to keep what they made.

How does the experience change by event type?

Choose your event first, Baby Shower, Engagement Party, Birthday, Holiday, or Special Moment, and the experiences, pricing, and guest gift you see are designed specifically for it.

What happens at the event?

Your selected experiences arrive prepared and ready. Throughout the celebration, guests play, write, photograph, and create using them.

What do guests actually do?

Depending on the experiences you choose, they play games, write notes, take photos, assemble puzzles, and leave messages and predictions behind.

What do I get to keep?

The keepsakes your guests create together: photos and notes, a finished storybook, assembled artwork, a filled time capsule, and more.

How do I receive everything?

Self Setup is for Toronto pickup or Toronto drop-off. Need delivery? Please contact us.

What does Self Setup mean?

Everything arrives prepared and ready for you to place and arrange yourself.

What does an Event Stylist do?

You don't lift a finger. We bring everything, set it up, style it, make sure every detail is ready, and take it all back when the celebration is over.

Can I add a Memory Display?

Yes. The Memory Display is independent from your service choice, self-styled or professionally styled, giving your keepsakes and experiences a beautiful focal point.

Can I add games?

Baby Shower builds already include Play & Connect games. Every other event type can add optional playful games in the builder for +$125 each.

What happens after I submit the builder?

We review your experience request and follow up to confirm availability, event details, and payment.
